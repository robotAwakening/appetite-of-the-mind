# Short video scripts (TikTok / Reels / Shorts, 20–40 s)

Film vertically, put captions on everything, and hook in the first 1.5 seconds. Put the link in your bio with the right `utm_source`.

## 1. The ask (launch)
- **0–2 s** (to camera, deadpan): "There are 60 million millionaires on Earth. I'm asking every single one of them for one euro."
- **2–10 s**: "I'm making my first album. No label. If they all say yes, that's 120 million euros. I need about [€X]."
- **10–20 s**: play 8 seconds of the best part of a track.
- **20–28 s**: "Not a millionaire? Same. You can still chip in, or just send this to one rich person you know."
- **On-screen text:** "Day 1: 0 / 60,000,000"

## 2. The scoreboard (repeat at every milestone)
- **0–2 s**: screen recording of the counter going up: "Day [N]. [X] people. [Y] countries."
- Read out 3 messages from the wall, one funny, one sweet, one from a far-away country.
- End: "Millionaires so far: unconfirmed. Keep going."

## 3. Calling one out (use sparingly, keep it friendly)
- "Dear [famous rich person], this is a formal request for €1." Show a formal-looking letter, sign it, put it in a bottle, etc.
- Make it obviously comedic. Tag them only in the public post, never in DMs.

## 4. Where the money goes
- Show the studio, the gear and the receipt for the session the first €[X] paid for. Transparency builds trust and converts.

## 5. The supporter credit
- Scroll through the growing list of names "going on the album". "Want your name in the credits of an album for one euro?"
