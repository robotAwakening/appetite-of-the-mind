FAQ

Fair questions.

Is this real?

Yes. A real musician, a real album, real money going to make it. The counter and the wall show actual payments, recorded straight from Stripe.

Do I actually have to be a millionaire?

Nope. The millionaires are the joke and the hook, but everyone's welcome. Most supporters are just people who think it's a fun idea.

What does the "I'm a millionaire" box do?

It gives you a gold star on the wall and adds you to the millionaire counter. It's purely on the honour system. Nobody checks, and nothing else changes.

What do I get for €2?

Your name in the album credits (if you want it), a spot on the public wall, and everything that unlocks as the campaign grows. That includes demos, live sessions and bonus tracks.

Where does the money go?

Into making the album: studio time, mixing and mastering, musicians, artwork and release. The full budget is on The Money page.

Is this an investment? Do I get a share of the album?

No. It's support, like buying a ticket to something you want to exist. There are no financial returns.

Is paying safe?

Payment happens on Stripe's secure checkout page. This site never sees your card details.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you gave by mistake, email me within 30 days and I'll refund it, no questions asked.

Will you email me?

Only if you subscribe yourself, and only at milestones. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe. I never buy or scrape email lists. See the privacy page.

Who's Risk of Rain?

That's me too. My first three songs came out under that name. Appetite of the Mind is the new name for the same project, now that it's growing into an album. Hear them here.

I'm a journalist. Can I write about this?

Please do! Everything you need is on the press page, including live numbers.
