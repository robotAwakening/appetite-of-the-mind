The story

Why ask
millionaires?

Because it's absurd, and because absurd things get noticed. And because €2 is the smallest amount that still means someone chose to back art.

Hi. I'm the Appetite.

I make music as Appetite of the Mind. I'm based in Finland, with a Spanish heart. I've put out three songs so far (as Risk of Rain), all made on a budget of roughly zero.

The next step is a full album, properly recorded, mixed and released. That costs real money, and I don't have a label, an investor or a rich uncle.

So I did the maths.

There are about 60 million millionaires in the world. If a tiny fraction of them each gave me the price of a bus ticket, the album would be paid for many times over.

So I'm asking. Publicly, honestly, with a live counter so everyone can watch whether it works. And since it would be weird to turn away non-millionaires, everyone's invited.

The rules I set myself

1. Everything public

The counter, the money, what it's spent on. If it fails, it fails in public too.

2. Everyone credited

Anyone who gives and wants their name on the album gets it there, whether they gave €2 or €200.

3. No spam

I don't cold-email anyone, I don't buy lists, and I won't pester you. You found this, or a friend sent it. That's the point.

“Art used to have patrons. Now it can have sixty million of them.”
