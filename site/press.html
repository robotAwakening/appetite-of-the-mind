Short bio

Appetite of the Mind is the project of a Finland-based musician with Spanish roots. Three self-produced singles came out under the earlier name Risk of Rain. The €1 campaign funds the first full album, and it's run entirely in public, as a funding experiment and an art piece about what the internet will pay for.
